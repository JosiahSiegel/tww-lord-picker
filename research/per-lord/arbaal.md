# Arbaal the Undefeated — Research Notes

## Identity
- **Name:** Arbaal the Undefeated
- **Race / Faction / Source:** Khorne / Challengers of Khorne / FLC (Update 6.0)
- **Difficulty (current):** 2

## Current App Content

**Tags:** `["Aggro","Mortal roster","Free"]`

**Mech:**
1. **Challenges of Khorne** — "Khorne marks armies across the map as challenges, graded from Worthy Battles up to Ultimate Bloodbaths. Winning banks Khorne's Favour, spent on toggleable Blessings — and Arbaal alone can teleport straight to a challenge to answer it in person"
2. **The Undefeated** — "lose a single battle with Arbaal and a dilemma fires: end the campaign on the spot, or carry on in disgrace as 'Arbaal the Defeated' with permanent penalties. A built-in ironman clause no other lord has"
3. **Khorne's chosen mortal** — "armies lean mortal — cheap, fast mortal recruitment and armoured warriors instead of daemon glass, so your line survives a bad engagement. Bloodletting decay still applies, as ever with Khorne"

**Pros:** "Free lord with a real campaign hook", "Mortal Khorne roster is tankier and more forgiving than daemons", "Northern start funnels you into juicy Kislev/Chaos brawls"

**Cons:** "Still no ranged answer", "Favour pressure punishes turtling", "The Defeated penalties are milder than the premise suggests — some call the ironman clause a paper tiger"

**Pick line:** "Pick if you want Khorne with armoured mortals instead of daemons, and you own no DLC."

### Faction effects (NEW from guide — significant additions not in app)

- **Upkeep -10% for non-daemonic units**
- **Construction cost -50% for mortal military and defense buildings**
- **Construction time -50% for mortal military and defense buildings**

These are major bonuses and should probably be added to a "faction effects" or expanded in mech[2].

### Victory conditions (NEW from guide)

- **Short:** Complete 8 Challenges of Khorne, occupy/loot/raze/sack 30 settlements. Reward: Chainsword.
- **Long:** 8+15 challenges, 60 settlements, 8 Skull Throne rituals, **destroy the Ecstatic Legions (Azazel)**. Reward: lord recruit rank +10.
