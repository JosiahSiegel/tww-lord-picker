## Steam Guide Verification (extract_content_from_websites)

**Source:** https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2965233791 (susaVile)

### Faction effects (verified)

- **Upkeep: -15% for armies led by Saurus Old-Bloods**
- **Saurus buildings grant additional bonuses to Saurus Scar-Veterans and Old-Bloods**
- **Weapon Strength +1% per character rank for Saurus Scar-Veterans and Old-Bloods (reaching +50% at max level)**
- **Experience gain +30% for Saurus Scar-Veterans and Saurus Old-Bloods**

### Victory conditions (verified)

- **Short:** Destroy Clan Morbidus, Clan Mordkin, The Silver Host, The Drakenhof Conclave (Mannfred), Leaf-Cutterz Tribe. 30 settlements. Reward: +3 hero capacity.
- **Long:** Short + 70 settlements + destroy Drakenhof Conclave, Exiles of Khorne (Skarbrand), Clan Mors (Queek), The Bloody Handz (Wurzzag), Harbinger of Disaster (Malagor), Poxmakers of Nurgle (Ku'gath), Oracles of Tzeentch (Kairos) + control 5 of 7 nearby Jungle provinces. Reward: +10 hero recruit rank.

### Starting location (verified)

"Middle of the road, highly defensible 2-settlement province near the sea. Begin in control of a minor settlement."

### Climate (verified)

"Average to bad. Aside from the nearby jungles, nearly all other climates are unpleasant or uninhabitable."

### Unique mechanics (verified)

- **Geomantic Web:** Acts upon and enhances province edicts. Requires specific buildings (taking up a slot in the province capital) and good relations with neighbors.
- **Rites:**
  - *Primeval Glory:* Summons an army of dinosaurs (costs upkeep) + army ability to summon Feral Cold Ones
  - *Rite of Sotek:* Causes attrition to enemies in your territory, +50% ambush chance, +10 melee attack for skinks, +15% weapon strength for skinks/chameleons, +20% missile strength for skink/terradon units
  - *Rite of Ferocity:* +80 unit XP per turn, +25% post-battle loot income, +2 recruit rank, +1 local recruit capacity
  - *Rite of Awakening:* Allows recruitment of a Slann Mage-Priest with different lores of magic
- **Blessed Spawnings:** Completing specific missions rewards "greater" versions of units added to a pool for instant recruitment.
- **Lord Kroak:** Access to an insanely powerful, unique spellcaster hero.
