### Faction effects (verified)
- Monster Pens: capture a variety of beasts and send them into battle
- Forces do not receive Vigour penalties from Raiding stance
- Upkeep: -10% for all monstrous units (all armies)

### Victory conditions (verified)
- Short: 30 settlements. Reward: +3 recruit rank to all units.
- Long: 75 settlements + control Shrine of Khaine + destroy main HE factions + control Eataine. Reward: +15% more income from slaves.
