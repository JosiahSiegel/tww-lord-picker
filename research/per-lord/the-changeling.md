# The Changeling — Research Notes

## Identity
- **Name:** The Changeling
- **Race / Faction / Source:** Tzeentch / The Deceivers / Shadows of Change
- **Difficulty (current):** 3

## Current App Content

**Tags:** `["No-conquest","Unique loop","Sneaky"]`

**Mech:**
1. **Trickster Cults** — "you don't own land; you plant cults. Settlements can host a Changeling-aligned cult that pays you gold, building slots, and other passive effects. It's a slow-burn economy with no defense — the cult is the city, and you move on"
2. **No Provinces, No Problem** — "you can't raze, can't capture. You send Heroes to plant Trickster Cults in settlements, draw income and effects, and disappear. The whole campaign is a slow creep of cult placement across the map"
3. **Tzeentch core** — "Changing of the Ways, Grimoires, all the standard Tzeentch trickster kit. The faction is Tzeentch without the territory game — pure campaign map manipulation"

**Pros:** "No-conquest gameplay is unique in the series", "Cult placement is the most 'different' campaign in WH3", "Sneaky, hideable, plays a totally different game"

**Cons:** "Defenseless if a cult is discovered", "Cannot expand territory — you don't have an empire", "The unique loop is not for everyone; players report frustration with the slow burn"

**Pick line:** "Pick if you want the most 'different' campaign in WH3 — cult-instead-of-conquest, hide and bleed your enemies."

### Faction effects (NEW from guide)

- **Immunity to diplomatic penalties from trespassing** (faction effect)
- **Can replenish and recruit in foreign territory** (faction effect)

### Victory conditions (NEW from guide)

- **Short:** Unlock and Win 2 Grand Scheme battles. Reward: +30 Winds of Magic reserve.
- **Long:** Short + Win 5 Grand Scheme battles + Win the Ultimate Scheme battle. Reward: +10 lord recruit rank.

### Climate

Guide: "In terms of climate... there is no climate. No worries about it, go anywhere and enjoy the landscapes that you like the most to fight on. However, this is not the faction to dominate the whole map with, given its mechanics."
