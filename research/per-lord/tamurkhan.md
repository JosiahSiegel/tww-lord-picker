# Tamurkhan the Maggot Lord — Research Notes

## Identity
- **Name:** Tamurkhan the Maggot Lord
- **Race / Faction / Source:** Nurgle / The Maggot Host / Thrones of Decay
- **Difficulty (current):** 2

## Current App Content

**Tags:** `["Warband","Council","Aggro Nurgle"]`

**Mech:**
1. **The Maggot Council** — "build a council of Nurgle-aligned characters (Tamurkhan + 4 Chieftains) and they all share progress. Get one buff, every lord on the council inherits it. A unique shared-progression mechanic that turns your faction into a real council-of-five warband instead of a normal four-lord stack"
2. **Chieftains of the Maggot Host** — "four unique Nurgle characters with their own quest battles and skill lines. Each one is functionally a Lord with a unique army, so effectively you get five 'main' lords — a real army of heroes, not a roster. Some of them are beast; some are situational"
3. **Nurgle core** — "Plagues + Infections + the post-Thrones rework symptom system. Same as Ku'gath but with the Chieftain twist"

**Pros:** "Council of five is a unique campaign hook", "Each Chieftain brings a different army flavor", "Nurgle durability stays strong"

**Cons:** "Some Chieftains are noticeably weaker than others", "Same Nurgle slowness as everyone", "Bloat in late game if you don't manage the council"

**Pick line:** "Pick if you want a Nurgle warband campaign where every lord is a hero — five unique armies, not a roster."
