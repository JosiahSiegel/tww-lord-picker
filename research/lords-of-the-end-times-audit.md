# Lords of the End Times — Research & Accuracy Audit

_Compiled 1 October 2026 for the TWW3 Lord Picker. Covers the paid DLC, the free
Update 9.0 content that shipped with it, and the community's agreed reading of both._

## TL;DR

- **Lords of the End Times** is the eighth _Total War: WARHAMMER III_ DLC, released
  **24 September 2026** — Creative Assembly's largest DLC in the series' 10-year history.
- It adds **4 paid Legendary Lords** plus the **free FLC Lord Neferata**, and — a series
  first — grants access to **Immortal Empires without owning any prior Total War: WARHAMMER game**.
- The paid lords are **Nagash** (new **Undead Legions** race, faction **Host of Nagash**),
  **Thanquol** (Skaven, **Clan Scruten**), **Boris Todbringer** (Empire, **Middenland**) and
  **the Glottkin** (Warriors of Chaos, **Host of the Triplets**).
- The accompanying **free Update 9.0** was one of the biggest in series history: a full
  **Vampire Counts rework**, an **Archaon rework** with Chaos-lord subjugation, three new
  **End Game scenarios**, and **overhauled victory conditions for every legacy Legendary Lord**.
- The picker now carries all five lords with corrected faction names, mechanics, heroes,
  difficulty and battle notes, plus the Update 9.0 ripple effects on existing entries.

## 2. The five End Times Legendary Lords

Confirmed against the CA blogs, the Total War: WARHAMMER Wiki and Wikipedia.

### 2.1 Nagash, Supreme Lord of the Undead — **Undead Legions / Host of Nagash**

- **Race:** Undead Legions (new). **Faction:** Host of Nagash. **Start:** Black Pyramid of
  Nagash, Great Mortis Delta. **Corruption:** Vampiric. **Currencies:** Dark Magic (income),
  Necromantic Energy (special).
- **Mechanics:** _Lands of the Dead_ (Necropolises gather Necromantic Energy from their own
  and every bordering province), **Gravecall** (raise units instantly anywhere in a province),
  the **Black Pyramid** (large Path-of-Exile-style research tree gated behind Empowered
  Sigils), **Mortarchs** (subjugate the marked Legendary Lords — Arkhan, Neferata, Luthor
  Harkon, Vlad and Mannfred — to inherit their empires), and the **Books of Nagash**.
- **Roster:** draws on **all** undead rosters (Tomb Kings + Vampire Counts + Vampire Coast);
  new units include Spirit Host, Drakenhof Templars, Morghast Harbingers/Archai, Khemric
  Titan and the Coven Throne.
- **Lores:** Lore of Undeath natively; the Books unlock extra spells from **Light, Death,
  Vampires and Nehekhara**.
- **Legendary Heroes:** Dieter Helsnicht, Walach Harkon, **and Krell** (Krell is shared with
  Kemmler's VC faction). The poltergeist follower **The Nameless** lets an assigned Lord
  possess enemy units pre-battle.
- **Battle:** an **apex caster _and_ melee lord** — CA calls him "probably the most powerful
  single character in the game".

### 2.2 Thanquol, the Grey Seer — **Skaven / Clan Scruten**

- **Mechanics:** _Masterplans_ — secret armies, dark rituals and heists, with complications
  and setbacks that must be resolved to realise the plan.
- **Roster:** full Skaven roster plus 4 Verminlord Lords, **six Stormfiend variants**
  (Grinderfists, Doom-flayer Gauntlets, Shock Gauntlets, Ratling Cannons, Windlaunchers,
  Warpfire Projectors), Pusbags, the Warp-Doom Magma-Cannon and the Cauldron of a Thousand
  Poxes. **Legendary Hero:** Skreech Verminking (notably a _hero_, while standard Verminlords
  are Lords — a community talking point). **Mount:** Boneripper.
- **Battle:** Grey Seer (Plague + Ruin lores, Dreaded Thirteenth spell) riding Boneripper —
  unusually durable for a Skaven caster.

### 2.3 Boris Todbringer, Elector Count of Middenland — **Empire / Middenland**

- **Mechanics:** _Great Temple of Ulric_ (Fervour → six Sanctuaries → the Flame of Ulric →
  Elector State Troopers), _Winter Rites_, and the **_Drakwald Threat_** (Beastmen pressure,
  with Khazrak One-Eye as personal nemesis).
- **Roster/LH:** **Legendary Hero Ar-Ulric Emil Valgeir**, plus Seneschal of the White Wolf,
  Priest of Ulric, Wolf-kin, Hunting Hounds, Warriors of Ulric, Teutogen Guard, Knights of the
  White Wolf, Knights Panther and the Celestial Hurricanum.
- **Battle:** fearsome melee combatant wielding **Legbiter**, with command bonuses to
  Middenland troops.

### 2.4 The Glottkin, the Befouled Brothers — **Warriors of Chaos / Host of the Triplets**

- **Faction name:** the **Host of the Triplets** (the CA blog's "Befouled Brothers" is an
  epithet, not the in-game faction). **Start:** Monolith of Katam, Helspire Mountains.
- **Mechanics:** _Gifts of Nurgle_ (Rituals of Decay / Grandfather's Blessings) and _Gardens
  of Corruption_ (high daemon-Nurgle access + plague spreading).
- **Legendary Heroes:** **four** — Gutrot Spume, Morbidex Twiceborn, Bloab Rotspawned and
  Orghotts Daemonspew.
- **Battle:** triple entity — Otto's scythe, Ethrac's sorcery, Ghurk's bulk.

### 2.5 Neferata, the Vampire Queen — **Vampire Counts (also flagged Undead Legions) / The Lahmian Sisterhood** — _free FLC_

- **Free** with Update 9.0; starts at the **Silver Pinnacle** (Blasted Wastes).
- **Signature mechanic:** **_Web of Power_** — Heroes plant hidden **Vampire Covens** in other
  factions' settlements to generate **Manipulation** and build **Concealment**, spent on
  assassination, theft, disruption and manipulation.
- **Lores:** Vampires / Shadows (wiki also lists Death). **Mounts:** Barded Nightmare,
  Hellsteed, Coven Throne, Dread Abyssal, Zombie Dragon.
- **Extras:** a Lahmia landmark lets her recruit a limited number of Tomb Kings units;
  **Ulrika Magdova** is unlocked via her technology tree.
- **Trivia:** Neferata was originally planned as the first _Character Pack_ LL; she was made
  FLC instead and **Bhashiva the White Tiger** took that slot.

## 3. The free Update 9.0 (shipped with the DLC)

- **Vampire Counts rework:** Raise Dead becomes the default, capacity-based recruitment.
  Tiers 1–3 cost **Corpses** (a provincial resource made by casualties); tiers 4–5 also cost
  **Blood**. **Vampire Lairs** must be discovered/developed to awaken Bloodline Lords, and
  **Bloodlines** can now be empowered endlessly. Confederation is gated behind tasks, plus new
  **Blood Decrees**. New Lahmian/Necrarch models and skill trees.
- **Archaon rework:** the Lord of the End Times can **subjugate** Chaos Legendary Lords
  (WoC, Khorne, Nurgle, Slaanesh, Tzeentch — not Beastmen/Chaos Dwarfs/Daemons/Norsca), and
  after Short Victory may choose a **Devastation** endgame instead of conquest.
- **End Game overhaul:** existing End Games became **Crisis Scenarios**, and three new,
  fully-authored **End Game scenarios** were added — **Chaos Invasion**, **Vermintide** and
  **Nagash Rises** — all using Devastation, music/lighting changes and cinematic buildups.
- **Victory conditions:** loreful Short/Long/Domination objectives for **every** legacy
  Legendary Lord, with per-LL Short Victory rewards.
- **Also:** Books of Nagash became carried, stealable items; **Daemonic Re-Emergence**;
  Escalating Wounds; **Sigvald** became a Slaanesh-focused lord; new Dark Fortresses and
  battle maps; end-turn time cut by up to 30%; a Regiments-of-Renown cap; new magic items
  (Settra, VC, the Red Duke) and much more.

## 4. Community consensus ("the community agreements")

Sourced from the CA forum comment threads under the official blogs, the subreddit, and the
Steam/Metacritic reception. Treat all as reported opinion, not CA statements.

1. **Faction names are settled.** A widely-upvoted r/totalwar thread ("Lords of the End
   Times playable factions name") and the wiki/Wikipedia agree: **Host of Nagash**, **Host of
   the Triplets**, **Clan Scruten**. The pre-release theory that Nagash would be faction-less
   like the Daemon Prince (Daniel) was **wrong**.
2. **Nagash is the agreed power fantasy.** CA itself sells him as "the most powerful single
   character in the game"; the community broadly accepts him as an easy, OP, map-painting
   campaign. The dissent is about **power creep**: a recurring worry that such a lord flattens
   campaign tension (a thread explicitly argues "if anyone deserves to be OP it's Nagash",
   countered by concern he'll be trivial by turn 15).
3. **The Vampire Counts rework is welcomed, with one reservation.** Broad approval for Lairs,
   Necrarch/Lahmian models and the capacity system; the recurring worry is that **zombie spam
   may still be the optimal build**, making the new toys redundant.
4. **The new End Game scenarios are seen as a genuine improvement** — a step up from
   army-spawning. Demands for future scenarios (e.g. a "good guy" scenario) already appear.
5. **Skaven roster disappointment.** The loudest complaint is the **absence of a Master
   Mutator and/or Plague Pontifex**, the only clans without themed generic lords. **Pusbags**
   were widely mocked as a wasted slot. Skreech Verminking being a hero rather than a lord was
   also frequently noted.
6. **Boris "sold twice" grumble.** Some Beastmen-DLC owners objected to paying again for
   Boris, who previously appeared as a Beastmen-faction character.
7. **Overall reception is mixed on storefronts, positive on content.** Reviews are
   mixed/negative recently (per PC Gamer/Gamereactor, in part review-bombing and unrelated
   grievances), while reviewers and the community generally rate the **content quality high**
   and the DLC the **largest in years**.

## 6. Picker feature — End Game scenarios are now first-class and filterable

Added to the site (`index.html`):

- A new **`ENDGAMES`** dataset holds the three Update 9.0 End Game Scenarios —
  **Chaos Invasion**, **Vermintide** and **Nagash Rises** — with a colour, tagline,
  summary, three-stage mechanic breakdown and the list of Legendary Lords caught up in each.
- A new **“End Game” chip row** (next to Race and Playstyle) filters the lord grid to the
  factions involved. Selecting a scenario also renders a full, expandable **scenario card**
  at the top of the results, so the scenarios are browsable content in their own right, not
  just tags.
- Lord cards gained clickable **◆ scenario chips** in their detail view, so you can jump from
  a lord into the scenario that involves them.
- Scenario names are indexed by the site **search** box.
- Scenario membership is derived from documented sources: Chaos Invasion = Archaon plus every
  Chaos-aligned Legendary Lord (the CA-listed subjugation roster); Vermintide = the Skaven
  Legendary Lords; Nagash Rises = Nagash plus his Mortarchs (Arkhan, Neferata, Luthor Harkon,
  Vlad, Mannfred).

## 8. Known uncertainties (do not over-assert)

- **Neferata's race** is listed by the wiki as **both Vampire Counts and Undead Legions**; the
  picker groups her under Vampire Counts (her faction, The Lahmian Sisterhood, is a VC faction)
  because the model's `r` field is single-valued.
- **Blood Kisses:** the wiki's Vampire Counts page still describes them, while the CA blog
  frames **Blood** as the rework currency spent on Bloodlines. The picker avoids claiming
  Blood Kisses were removed.
- **Difficulty** is subjective and inferred from consensus, not from CA ratings.
- **Lucrezzia / other rumoured lords:** none exist; the DLC is exactly 4 paid + 1 FLC.
