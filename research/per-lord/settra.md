## Current App Content

**Tags:** `["Army caps","No upkeep","Emperor"]`

**Mech:**
1. **No Upkeep** — "free armies — recruitment is capped, but you never pay a gold coin to maintain what you have. The combination of recruitment caps + free upkeep is unique; no other faction in the game has it"
2. **Canopic Jars & Mortuary Cult** — "raise better troops after battles and through Mortuary Cult buildings — but quality caps at T3 for the basic units. T4+ is locked behind Lord levels and tech, which means your late-game is bottlenecked on Lords, not money"
3. **The King of Kings** — "victory requires the Burial Pyramid + five other provinces with the right buildings, and the long victory expands the same. A campaign around a fixed sacred geography — the 'Khemri Tetralogy' in the southlands is the goal, not world conquest"

**Pros:** "Free upkeep lets you out-mass everyone lategame", "Strong archers, chariots, and constructs", "Fixed victory geography makes for a focused campaign"

**Cons:** "Recruitment caps mean you can't spam armies", "T4+ units are gated behind Lords and tech — slow early pace", "Climate restricts expansion options"

**Pick line:** "Pick if you want a slow, careful empire — build up to T5 slowly, then out-mass everyone with free upkeep."
