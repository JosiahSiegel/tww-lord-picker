## Current App Content

**Tags:** `["Plagues","Slow","Attrition"]`

**Mech:**
1. **Infections & Plagues** — "battles and certain hero actions drop Infections; you spend them to brew Plagues with three symptoms each, applied to settlements/armies. The cyclical building mechanic is uniquely Nurgle: you upgrade a building slot by slot, no skipping, and the system is satisfyingly gross in execution. Plague-bombing your enemy's economy is a real strategy"
2. **Nurgling Master** — "Nurglings come cheaper, come back healthier, and Ku'gath's faction literally has +10 growth. The little guys are his signature — you can build a real Nurgling stack if you want"
3. **Nurgle core** — "Plagues + Cults + Unholy Manifestations + the Daemons-of-Chaos roster. Same as Ku'gath's other faction except Ku'gath leans into Nurglings"

**Pros:** "Plague system is the most fun attrition-fest in the game", "Nurgle's toughness plays into the long, slow campaign", "Plague-bombing settlements is a real strategic lever"

**Cons:** "Slow army movement + slow economy = slow campaign", "Mountain terrain is unpleasant for Ku'gath (he doesn't like sand, basically)", "Skaven and Ogre outposts become a near-requirement for missile/anti-large"

**Pick line:** "Pick if you want a slow, attrition-fest Nurgle campaign — Plagues make you the worst neighbor, on purpose."
