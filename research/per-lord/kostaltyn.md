**Mech:**

**Cons:** "Low-tier troops fall off hard lategame", "Same Kislev hybrid-unit cost problems", "Less interesting hero game than the Ice Court"

### Faction effects (NEW from guide)

The guide lists specific Kostaltyn benefits NOT in the app:
- **-40 diplomatic relations with the Ice Court** (faction effect)
- **+10 Devotion generated from Patriarch hero actions**
- **Ward save +20% for Patriarchs**
- **Melee attack +5 and Leadership +5 for Kossars, Kossovite Dervishes, Horse Archers**

### Victory conditions (NEW from guide)

- **Short:** 600 Supporters, destroy Clan Moulder and Ecstatic Legions, 30 settlements. Reward: +3 hero capacity.
- **Long:** 70 settlements, destroy Warhost of the Apocalypse, Legions of Chaos, Fecundities. Reward: +10 hero recruit rank.
