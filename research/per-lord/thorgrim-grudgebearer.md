# Thorgrim Grudgebearer — Research Notes

## Identity
- **Name:** Thorgrim Grudgebearer
- **Race / Faction / Source:** Dwarfs / Karaz-a-Karak / WH1 base
- **Difficulty (current):** 2

## Current App Content

**Tags:** `["Grudges","Defensive","Economy"]`

**Mech:**
1. **Grudge System** — "the Grudge book accumulates a list of slights — enemies that took Dwarfs lands, killed Dwarfs lords, etc. Settling a Grudge pays gold and faction-wide buffs. Sully the Book and you get debuffs. The Grudge book is the most unique Dwarfs mechanic — every campaign is partially reactive"
2. **High King Authority** — "Dwarfs can confederate the Holds and decree laws. Thorgrim is the High King by default, so you start with the most Authority and the most Decree power. A slow political game that comes online around turn 30"
3. **Dwarfs core** — "Grudge Settler units, Oathgold crafting, the new Age of Reckoning, the deepest crafting system in the game. T3/T4/T5 unlocks depend on the Hold you confederate"

**Pros:** "The classic Dwarfs campaign — most iconic starting position", "Most Authority of any Dwarfs lord, so decrees are easy to stack", "Best economy in the game (once you get it rolling)"

**Cons:** "Grudge book management is fiddly", "Slow start before you confederate the Holds", "Climate is rough outside the Badlands and mountains"

**Pick line:** "Pick if you want the iconic Dwarfs campaign — Grudges, Hold confederation, the slow build to a great empire."
