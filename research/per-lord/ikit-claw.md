## Current App Content

**Tags:** `["Nukes","Workshop","Fan favourite"]`

**Mech:**
1. **The Forbidden Workshop** — "Ikit's unique building chain, a Skryre research/upgrades facility that unlocks Warp Lightning Cannons, Doom-Flayers, Doomrockets (literal nukes), and Regiment of Renown Skryre units. The Workshop progresses via Warp Fuel, a unique currency you earn from battles and missions. By lategame your army has stats mods you can't get from any other Skaven"
2. **Warp Fuel & Research Rate** — "+20% research rate, +2 loyalty for new recruits, -40% engineering construction. The Workshop economy runs in parallel with normal Skaven research — both are accelerated"
3. **Skaven core** — "Under-Cities, Doomstack of the Month, Vermintide. Same Skaven strengths; Ikit just gets a different skill tree (the Workshop) that lets the Skryre roster sing"

**Pros:** "Most fun Skaven in the game — the Workshop progression is the campaign's highlight", "Doomrockets are literal nukes in battle", "+20% research rate is real value"

**Cons:** "Requires more attention to Warp Fuel than other Skaven", "Warp Fuel is gated behind battles/missions, can be slow", "The 1-settlement start is the most defensible but also the smallest"

**Pick line:** "Pick if you want the most workshop-driven Skaven — Doomrockets and Warp Fuel are the campaign's payoff."
