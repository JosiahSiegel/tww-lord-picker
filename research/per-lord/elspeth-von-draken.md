# Elspeth von Draken — Research Notes

## Identity
- **Name:** Elspeth von Draken
- **Race / Faction / Source:** The Empire / Wissenland & Nuln / Thrones of Decay
- **Difficulty (current):** 2

## Current App Content

**Tags:** `["Gunline","Upgrades","Strong"]`

**Mech:**
1. **Schematics & Master Engineers** — "every gun you kill yields a schematic; trading schematics at Drakenhof upgrades your gunpowder game. Combined with a Master Engineer lord (grenade launcher lord with research + buffs) it's a feedback loop that snowballs your artillery and guns. A different Empire experience from the all-rounder Karl Franz"
2. **Castle Drakenhof** — "a vampire-occupied castle is your capital and the unique building chain. After 8 turns the vampire rebels collapse and Drakenhof is yours. Ten slots, a real home base — Empire armies built around it, and the gun-schematic economy is the rare Empire mechanic that asks you to think"
3. **Vlad's neighbour** — "you start next to Vlad von Carstein, who will absolutely declare war and try to take your lands. If you don't kill him fast, he scales. The opposite of an 'easy Empire start'"

**Pros:** "Gun-heavy army comps punch well above their weight", "Schematic economy is the most interesting Empire mechanic", "T3 Knights of the Black Rose from Gardens of Morr are busted early"

**Cons:** "Vlad is a real early threat if you hesitate", "Vampire Coast / dark-elf aggression late game", "Strong on offence, weaker on defense if your line breaks"

**Pick line:** "Pick if you want the gun-and-grenades Empire variant — strongest when played aggressively from turn 1."
