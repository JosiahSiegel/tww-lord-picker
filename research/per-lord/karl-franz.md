## Current App Content

**Tags:** `["Politics","All-rounder","Beginner-friendly"]`

**Mech:**
1. **Elector Counts** — "every state capital gets its assigned Elector Count, stacking buffed troops and a free summon-once. Lose a capital, lose the Count. A real territorial hook — and one of the few factions where the map itself is the resource"
2. **Imperial Authority** — "a slow-burning political bar: high authority buys the Emperor real Imperium-wide buffs, low authority starts chipping away. The bar doesn't tick fast, so most players ignore it after the early game — by design"
3. **Empire core** — "Demigryphs, Greatswords, Steam Tanks, and a roster that's literally designed to be a 'starter faction' tutorial by example. Steam Tank and Luminark unlock at T3, which is one of the fastest elite-unit unlocks in the game"

**Pros:** "Most well-rounded roster in the game — a real 'play anything' faction", "Elector Counts make every conquered city a small power-up", "Great artillery, infantry, cavalry, ranged — every box is checked"

**Cons:** "Climate is the worst in the game — never ownable without penalties", "Authority grind is mostly background noise", "Boring mid-game if you don't have a goal in mind"

**Pick line:** "Pick if you want the safest, most flexible campaign — the recommended first play for Total War Warhammer beginners."
