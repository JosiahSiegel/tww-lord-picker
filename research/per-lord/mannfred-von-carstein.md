# Mannfred von Carstein — Research Notes

## Identity
- **Name:** Mannfred von Carstein
- **Race / Faction / Source:** Vampire Counts / The Drakenhof Conclave / WH1 base
- **Difficulty (current):** 2

## Current App Content

**Tags:** `["Necromancy","Flexible","Classic"]`

**Mech:**
1. **Books of Nagash** — "scattered around the world, these artifacts give permanent faction-wide buffs. Castle Drakenhof has a unique building that doubles the Books' effects. A long-term treasure hunt that pays off massively once you find them all"
2. **Bloodlines & Blood Kisses** — "Blood Kisses unlock Bloodline Lords, the named-vampire generals. Same as other VC lords, but Mannfred starts with 1 Kiss and a +20% research rate that compounds the entire campaign. The vampire roster is a true roster of named characters, not a faction of generic skeletons"
3. **Vampire Counts core** — "Raise Dead, Vampiric Corruption, no ranged, magic-heavy. The classic vampire playstyle, dated but solid"

**Pros:** "The most flexible Vampire Counts faction", "Books of Nagash is the most flavorful VC mechanic", "Research rate +20% lets you tech up faster than other VC lords"

**Cons:** "Classic VC weaknesses (no ranged, slow economy)", "Climate is poor — too many climates are penalizing", "Long victory requires you to control all Empire provinces, which is awkward from a Southlands start"

**Pick line:** "Pick if you want the classic Vampire Counts experience — magic and Raise Dead at its purest."
