### Faction effects (NEW from guide)

- **Asur Domination mechanic** (unique)
- **Can issue provincial commandments without owning the full province**
- **Improved sea navigation (immunity to attrition, free sea lane travel)**
- **Mist Landing stance** (move sea → land instantly, ignore terrain)
- **Local recruitment duration -1 turn for Oceanids, Sea Elementals, and Merwyrm units**

### Victory conditions (NEW from guide)

- **Short:** 30 settlements, 3 Dragonships, 3 Colonies, destroy Dread Rock Privateers, restore 3 Elven Colonies. Reward: +3 hero capacity.
- **Long:** Short + destroy Dread Rock Privateers, Awakened (Luthor Harkon), Pirates of Sartosa (Aranessa), Blessed Dead (Lokhir) + restore 9 Ancient Elven Colonies + win "The Lost Cache" final battle. Reward: +20% movement, +10 hero rank, -20% Dragonship supply construction cost.
