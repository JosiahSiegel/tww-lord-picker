## Current App Content

**Tags:** `["Duelist","Politics","Beginner-friendly"]`

**Mech:**
1. **Defender of Ulthuan** — "every captured High Elf settlement spawns a Rebellion, by design. A ritual loop that forces you to keep your home province loyal; conquering outside it is encouraged, holding what you take inside it is the actual challenge. Great mechanical hook for roleplay; mid-tier if you only care about wins"
2. **The Phoenix King** — "an internal political arena (Court of the Phoenix King) where six High Elf factions jockey for control, and the dominant one can pick a global bonus. Tyrion's faction sits at the centre of the political machine and the bonus stack is meaningful"
3. **High Elf core** — "the well-rounded HE roster with the best archers and anti-large in the game, plus strong dragon princes. Slann are weird, but dragons are the most common combatant"

**Pros:** "Best archers in the game (Bolt Throwers + Sisters of Avelorn)", "Strong dragons and Dragon Princes for the burst damage", "Beginner-friendly with a forgiving HE roster"

**Cons:** "Internal politics + rebellions add management overhead", "Most factions outside Ulthuan hate you", "Roster can feel one-note (ranged + dragons)"

**Pick line:** "Pick if you want the safest HE start and a forgiving ranged-and-dragon playstyle."
